# Voice-to-Text Desktop App — Personas

## Overview
Personas for a voice-to-text note application that captures audio from microphone or recordings of others, transcribing them into editable notes. MVP target: learners and knowledge workers who prioritize speaking over typing.

---

## Persona 1: Alex (Student)
**Priority:** High

### Background
A university student who attends lectures and studies educational material. Alex speaks quickly but types slowly, creating a bottleneck when trying to capture lectures and study content in real-time.

### Problem
- Typing during lectures causes Alex to miss content or fall behind
- Transcribing notes manually after lectures is time-consuming
- Notes need to be accurate for exam preparation

### How They Use It
- Records lectures during class with a single-click start
- Captures explanations from study group members and tutors
- Reviews transcribed notes immediately after class to identify gaps
- Corrects transcription errors by typing while studying
- Re-records unclear sections to add context
- Keeps notes saved for later exam review

### Key Motivation
Speaking faster than typing allows Alex to keep pace with lectures and stay mentally engaged with material.

---

## Persona 2: Jordan (Professional Learner)
**Priority:** High

### Background
A working professional pursuing certifications, training programs, or self-directed learning. Jordan has limited free time for study but is committed to professional development.

### Problem
- Typing interrupts the learning flow during limited study sessions
- Notes need to be polished for potential sharing or professional reference
- Needs to build a searchable knowledge base from multiple courses

### How They Use It
- Records thoughts while watching course videos without pausing to type
- Captures insights from mentors and colleagues on technical topics
- Refines notes to be grammatically correct and professionally formatted
- Saves notes across multiple courses to build a personal knowledge base
- Returns to notes for professional reference and decision-making

### Key Motivation
Minimizing typing friction allows Jordan to complete professional learning on a compressed schedule while maintaining quality.

---

## Persona 3: Sam (Speed Mismatch)
**Priority:** Medium

### Background
Someone whose natural speaking speed significantly exceeds typing ability. Sam has a thick or distinctive accent but speaks clearly and intentionally.

### Problem
- The keyboard is a bottleneck to capturing thoughts at speaking pace
- Re-typing or correcting text defeats the purpose of faster capture
- Needs a workflow that keeps the flow going without friction

### How They Use It
- Records thoughts in real-time without keyboard delays
- Captures explanations from others for later reference
- Makes quick corrections to generated notes through typing or re-recording
- Accesses notes frequently throughout the day for quick reference

### Key Motivation
Removing typing friction enables Sam to operate at their natural speed and capture ideas before they're lost.

---

## Commonalities Across Personas
- All three speak English as a primary language (accents present but not a barrier)
- All use the app frequently, almost daily
- All value the ability to record both their own voice and others' voices
- All need the flexibility to edit notes either by typing or voice dictation
- All expect notes to persist and be retrievable across sessions
