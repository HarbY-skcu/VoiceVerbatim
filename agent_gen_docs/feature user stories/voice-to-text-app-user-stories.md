# Voice-to-Text Desktop App — User Stories

## Overview
User stories for a voice-to-text note application MVP. These stories are organized by persona and follow the user journey from recording to saving notes.

---

## Alex (Student) — User Stories

### Story 1: Record Lecture Audio Quickly
**As Alex**  
**I want** to start recording with a single click  
**So I can** capture the full lecture without missing content

**Satisfaction Condition:** A "Record" button is visible and active immediately when the app opens; recording starts within 1 second of clicking

---

### Story 2: Capture Peer and Tutor Explanations
**As Alex**  
**I want** to record my study group members or tutors  
**So I can** review their explanations when studying alone

**Satisfaction Condition:** The app records clearly regardless of whose voice is speaking; multi-person conversations are captured intelligibly

---

### Story 3: Review Lecture Notes Immediately After Class
**As Alex**  
**I want** to see the transcribed text of my lecture recording right away  
**So I can** identify gaps or unclear sections while the material is still fresh

**Satisfaction Condition:** Text appears within seconds of recording ending; it's readable and accurately reflects the lecture content

---

### Story 4: Fix Transcription Errors While Studying
**As Alex**  
**I want** to correct transcription mistakes by typing  
**So I can** have accurate notes for exam preparation

**Satisfaction Condition:** Alex can click any word/phrase, edit it, and see the change reflected immediately

---

### Story 5: Add Missing Context to Notes
**As Alex**  
**I want** to re-record a section or dictate clarifications using voice  
**So I can** fill gaps without breaking my study rhythm

**Satisfaction Condition:** Alex can select a section, re-record it, and replace the original transcription

---

### Story 6: Keep Notes for Later Review
**As Alex**  
**I want** my lecture notes saved and accessible weeks later  
**So I can** study without losing my work

**Satisfaction Condition:** Notes persist across sessions; Alex can open any saved note from previous lectures

---

## Jordan (Professional Learner) — User Stories

### Story 7: Capture Course Content Without Typing Friction
**As Jordan**  
**I want** to record my thoughts while watching course videos  
**So I can** stay engaged with the material and complete learning in limited free time

**Satisfaction Condition:** Recording and playback are seamless; no typing required during initial capture

---

### Story 8: Record Mentor or Colleague Insights
**As Jordan**  
**I want** to capture advice and explanations from colleagues  
**So I can** reference their insights when applying what I learned

**Satisfaction Condition:** App clearly captures professional conversations with technical terminology; multiple speakers are distinguishable

---

### Story 9: Review and Polish Notes for Professional Use
**As Jordan**  
**I want** to refine the transcribed text to be error-free  
**So I can** confidently reference or share these notes

**Satisfaction Condition:** Edited notes are grammatically correct and professionally formatted; text editing is straightforward

---

### Story 10: Build a Personal Knowledge Base
**As Jordan**  
**I want** to save and organize notes from multiple courses  
**So I can** grow my professional knowledge over time

**Satisfaction Condition:** Multiple notes are saved and retrievable; notes from different sessions remain distinct and accessible

---

## Sam (Speed Mismatch) — User Stories

### Story 11: Capture Thoughts Faster Than Typing
**As Sam**  
**I want** to record my thoughts in real-time  
**So I can** get ideas down at my natural speaking pace

**Satisfaction Condition:** Recording captures speech naturally; no lag or friction between thinking and capturing

---

### Story 12: Record Others' Input
**As Sam**  
**I want** to record what someone explains to me  
**So I can** reference their input later

**Satisfaction Condition:** App transparently records any voice input with good clarity

---

### Story 13: Make Quick Corrections to Generated Text
**As Sam**  
**I want** to fix errors by typing or re-recording sections  
**So I can** have usable notes without extensive editing

**Satisfaction Condition:** Both typing and voice editing are quick, single-action operations; minimal friction

---

### Story 14: Access Notes Whenever I Need Them
**As Sam**  
**I want** all my captured notes saved and instantly retrievable  
**So I can** reference them without friction

**Satisfaction Condition:** Notes are always available and organized logically for quick recall
